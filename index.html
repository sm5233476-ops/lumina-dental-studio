FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers to Your Clinical & Financial Questions.

Everything you need to know about our comfort protocols, implant timelines, and private concierge admissions.

Twilight sedation is administered and continuously monitored by board-certified dental anesthesiologists using hospital-grade vital monitoring equipment. It provides profound relaxation and analgesia, and many patients recall very little of the procedure once completed.

Using 3D computer-guided planning, temporary provisional teeth are frequently attached the same day of surgery, allowing you to leave with an immediate functional smile. Final monolithic zirconia bridges are placed after biological integration, typically 3 to 6 months later.

We provide flexible healthcare financing options, including 0% APR plans for up to 24 months, as well as extended 36- and 48-month options for qualified applicants. All quotes are transparent with zero hidden clinical facility fees.

Our studio was designed from the ground up to eradicate clinical fear. Private soundproof suites, noise-cancelling headphones, ceiling entertainment, gentle computerized local numbing, and twilight sleep sedation ensure an entirely stress-free visit.

Crafted from medical-grade lithium disilicate or feldspathic porcelain by master ceramists, veneers routinely perform for 15 to 20 years or longer with conscientious oral hygiene and regular preventive cleanings.

We work with most major PPO dental plans as an out-of-network provider. Our concierge billing desk completes and files all claims directly on your behalf to help you maximize your allowable out-of-network benefits.
